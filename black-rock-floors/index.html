FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still wondering about something? Call or text (425) 543-4667 and you’ll get a straight answer.

What flooring do you install?

Hardwood (solid and engineered), luxury vinyl plank, laminate and tile: porcelain, ceramic and natural stone. We also refinish existing hardwood and build stair treads, risers and nosing to match.

What does a new floor cost?

It depends on three things: the material you choose, the square footage and what the subfloor needs. After a free in-home measure we send a written proposal with scope, materials, price and schedule, usually within 24 hours. The number you approve is the number you pay.

Can you refinish my existing hardwood instead of replacing it?

Usually, yes. Solid hardwood can be sanded and refinished several times, and many engineered floors can be refinished at least once if the wear layer is thick enough. We check the boards during the measure and tell you honestly if refinishing makes sense.

Do you install over concrete slabs or basements?

Yes. We test the slab for moisture first, then recommend a floor that suits it, usually luxury vinyl plank, engineered hardwood or tile, with the right vapor barrier and leveling underneath.

How long does an install take?

Most single rooms take one to two days. A full floor of a house usually takes three to five working days. Hardwood may need a few days to acclimate in your home before we start. Your written proposal includes the schedule.

Do you move the furniture and haul away the old floor?

Tear-out and haul-away of the old flooring are part of the job, and we can move standard furniture. Please clear small items, and tell us ahead of time about pianos, safes or anything fragile.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM to 2 PM. Closed on Sundays.

Where do you offer services?

Across King and Snohomish County: Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Bothell, Sammamish, Issaquah, Renton, Woodinville, Kenmore, Shoreline, Everett, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Mill Creek, Mukilteo, Marysville, Monroe, Snohomish and the cities around them.
