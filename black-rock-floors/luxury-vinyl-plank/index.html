Good to know

Before you choose luxury vinyl plank.

Best rooms
Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, basements, rentals and any home with pets. It’s quiet, warm underfoot and easy to clean.
Wear layer
Thicker wear layers hold up better. Around 12 mil suits light traffic; 20 mil is the pick for busy homes, kids and dogs.
Flat matters
Vinyl shows every dip in the subfloor, so leveling is part of the job, not an extra we find later.
Waterproof planks
The planks are waterproof. Standing water should still be wiped up so it can’t reach the subfloor at the edges.

FAQ

Luxury Vinyl Plank questions

Still wondering about something? Call or text (425) 543-4667 and you’ll get a straight answer.

Is luxury vinyl plank really waterproof?

Rigid-core LVP planks are waterproof, which makes them a great fit for kitchens, baths and basements. Standing water should still be wiped up so it can’t reach the subfloor at the edges.

Click-lock or glue-down?

Click-lock floats over the subfloor and goes in fast. Glue-down is better for large open areas, heavy rolling loads or rooms with big temperature swings, like sunrooms. We recommend one after the measure.

Can LVP go over my existing floor?

It can often go over a flat, well-bonded floor like sheet vinyl or tile. Carpet always comes out. We check during the measure.
