The studio

Italian roots. Brazilian hands. Florida homes.

NUVO is led by master craftsman Eduardo Ramos: of Italian descent, born in Brazil and raised in a family of carpenters in Belo Horizonte.

After more than three decades at the bench, he brought that heritage to South Florida in 2015. Today Eduardo and his partner Renata run a team that pairs CNC precision with old-world hand finishing, and works side by side with architects, designers, builders and homeowners.

Every project is founder-led, from the first visit to the final hinge.

How we work

From the first measure to the last hinge.

  1. 01

    Visit & measure

    We review your project, visit the home and take every measurement on site.

  2. 02

    3D design & proposal

    You see the room in 3D, with a detailed quote for materials, finishes and timeline. The consultation is free.

  3. 03

    Agreement

    Scope, materials and schedule are signed off before anything is cut.

  4. 04

    Production

    Shop drawings, CNC precision and hand finishing, with quality checks at every stage.

  5. 05

    Delivery & installation

    Professional installation in your home, with the same care for the last detail.

  6. 06

    Aftercare

    Care guidance, support after installation and a 36-month warranty.
