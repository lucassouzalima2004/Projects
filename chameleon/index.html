How it works

From sketch to switch-on

Four steps, one team, no surprises. You always know what is being made and when it goes up.

  1. 01

    Brief & site survey

    Tell us what you need and send a photo. We visit, measure and check the wall, window or vehicle.

  2. 02

    Design & proof

    You see the sign on your building, or the wrap on your van, before anything is made. Changes are free until you approve.

  3. 03

    Build & print

    Letters are cut, built and lit; graphics are printed and laminated. Everything is switched on and checked before it leaves.

  4. 04

    Install & switch on

    Fitted cleanly and safely, at height when needed, with the site left tidy. Then the best part: lights on.
