      Our story

      More than great coffee.

      The Berry Coffee is a little café on Arklow’s Main Street with a big welcome. Plants in the window, good music in the kitchen and a seat for everyone: the kind of place you pop into for a flat white and end up staying for brunch.

      Chef George runs the kitchen, cooking proper Irish breakfasts, brunch and a new menu every season. Thiago and the team look after the counter, the coffee and the playlist. We brought a little Brazilian sunshine to Co. Wicklow, and Arklow made us feel at home from the very beginning.

      Thiago & George
