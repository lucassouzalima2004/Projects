FAQ

Good questions.

Can’t see yours? Ask us, we’re happy to help.

How much does window cleaning cost?

It depends on how many windows you have, the number of storeys and access, and whether you’d like inside, outside, tracks and flyscreens done. Send us a few details (photos help) and we’ll come back with a free quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. For outside-only cleans we just need safe access: gates unlocked and pets inside. For inside windows or a full clean, someone can let us in, or we can arrange access with you beforehand.

How often should windows be cleaned?

Most homes look their best with a clean every three to six months. Near the coast, a busy road or a building site, every two to three months stops salt and dust from building up.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Adelaide and clean homes and businesses across the metro area. Not sure if we come to you? Just ask.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes. We arrive with everything we need and use trusted, high-quality products for a deep, effective clean every time.

How does the bond-back guarantee work?

If your bond depends solely on our end-of-lease cleaning, we guarantee you’ll get it back. We clean to real estate standards, so the property is ready for the final inspection.

Can you clean my home or office regularly?

Yes. Regular cleans are tailored to your schedule, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, and we also do one-off and spring cleans whenever you need them.
