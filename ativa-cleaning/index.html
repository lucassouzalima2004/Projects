FAQ

Good questions.

Can’t see yours? Ask us, we’re happy to help.

How much does a clean cost?

Regular house cleans start from $53. Every home and business is different, so we quote on the size of the space, its condition and what you need. Send us a few details (photos help) and we’ll come back with a free quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Many clients leave us a key or an access code and come home to a clean house. We’ll arrange access with you before the first clean.

When can you clean my business?

Tell us your opening hours and the times that suit you, and we’ll find a schedule that works for your team and your customers.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes. We arrive with everything we need and use trusted, high-quality products for a deep, effective clean every time.

How does the bond-back guarantee work?

If your bond depends solely on our end-of-lease cleaning, we guarantee you’ll get it back. We clean to real estate standards, so the property is ready for the final inspection.

Can you clean regularly?

Yes. Regular cleans are tailored to your schedule, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, for homes and workplaces. We also do once-off and spring cleans whenever you need them.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Adelaide and clean homes and businesses across the metro area. Not sure if we come to you? Just ask.
