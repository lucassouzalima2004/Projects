Why film, not a refit

Don’t rip it out. Wrap it.

When the layout still works, a full refit is mostly paying to throw things away. Architectural film puts a new, durable finish over the desks, walls, doors and joinery you already have, so you get the new look without the demolition bill, the skip bins or the weeks of lost trade.

Good questions

Before you ask.

Got plans, a deadline or a building manager to keep happy? Send it all on WhatsApp and we’ll reply.

Ask on WhatsApp
Can you work around our trading hours?

Yes. We can plan the install after hours, on weekends or in stages, so customers and staff are disrupted as little as possible.

Is the film fire rated?

BODAQ architectural film is tested to ASTM E84 and carries a Class A fire and smoke rating from the manufacturer. Tell us what your building requires and we’ll provide the product documentation.

Can you match our brand colours?

With hundreds of solid colours, timbers, stones, metals and fabrics in the range, we can usually get very close. You’ll see real samples on site before anything is ordered.

Can you wrap lifts?

Yes: lift doors, wall panels and cars can all be wrapped. We check the surfaces and any building requirements with your building manager first.

How long does it last?

Architectural film is made for high-traffic spaces. The film carries a 10-year manufacturer warranty, and our installation is guaranteed for 2 years.

What does a commercial job cost?

Every space is different: area, surfaces, access and timing all play a part. Send photos or plans and we’ll come back with a clear, fixed quote, usually a fraction of what a refit would cost.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Welland and cover Adelaide and surrounding areas. For larger projects further afield, just ask.
